Resources

Government sources, case trackers, immigration-law blogs, and community forums.

These are the official government pages and trusted references behind this tool. Everything below opens in a new tab; nothing is fetched or saved for you.

Each source is tagged Official (U.S. government), Secondary (law firm or third-party tool), or Community (crowd-reported, not verified).

Official Government Sources

Visa Bulletin

Official

Monthly cutoff dates for all EB categories. Check this to see whether your priority date is current.

travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/legal/visa-law0/visa-bulletin.html

USCIS Processing Times

Official

Current processing times for I-140, I-485, etc.. Use it to gauge how long a filed form is taking.

egov.uscis.gov/processing-times/

DOL PERM Processing

Official

PERM and PWD processing timelines. Check this for the labor-certification step before I-140.

flag.dol.gov/

USCIS Policy Manual

Official

Official USCIS policy guidance for immigration benefits.

www.uscis.gov/policy-manual

Federal Register

Official

New rules, proposed rules, and policy changes. Watch this for changes that could affect your case.

www.federalregister.gov/
